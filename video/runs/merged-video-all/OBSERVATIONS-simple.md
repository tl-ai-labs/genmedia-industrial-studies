# What the videos look like: Omni Flash (Google) vs Seedance 2.5 (ByteDance)

1. **Both models make professional-looking videos.** Lighting, sharpness and product shots look like a real studio shoot in most clips.
2. **Seedance keeps the product label exactly right.** Omni Flash often changes or garbles the small print, such as the pack weight or the line under the brand name.
3. **Big text is reliable on both.** On-screen lines like "NEW - 40% MORE", "BREW BETTER" and a shop sign stay spelled correctly and steady.
4. **Hands are the weakest point for both.** Fingers slide, blur or melt together when opening a jar or wiping a surface; Seedance's hands looked worse on the jar-opening ad.
5. **Smooth camera moves look natural on both.** Product rotations, slow push-ins and pans run steadily without wobble.
6. **Omni Flash sometimes shifts the scene mid-clip.** In the before-and-after cleaning ad, the sink and window moved while the wipe crossed the frame.
7. **Simple edits look real on both.** A vase added to a table, with its shadow, and a slow zoom on a neon sign are hard to tell from the original footage.
8. **Omni Flash's colour change was spotless.** It turned a red umbrella yellow with no flicker as it moved.
9. **Cutting a person onto a new background is weak on both.** You can see rough edges and a glow round the person; Seedance's edges flicker more.
10. **Seedance's added footage doesn't match the original.** When it made a clip longer, the walk jumped at the join and the new part turned blurry.

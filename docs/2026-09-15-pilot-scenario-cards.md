## Game briefs

### BGMI

| Heading | Brief |
|---|---|
| **How a match works** | Up to 100 players board a cargo plane that flies across an island. Each player picks when to jump, parachutes down and lands where they choose. They loot houses for weapons, armour, medical items and scopes. A circular safe zone (the play zone) shrinks in stages and anyone outside it loses health, which pushes players together. A red zone occasionally bombs a random area. Airdrop crates fall under a parachute with red smoke and carry rare weapons and a ghillie suit. The last player or squad standing wins and sees the 'Winner Winner Chicken Dinner' screen. A match lasts 25 to 30 minutes. There are also short Team Deathmatch and Arena modes. |
| **Core actions** | Jumping from the plane and steering the parachute. Looting weapons and gear from floors and crates. Aiming down sights with scopes (red dot, 2x, 3x, 4x, 6x, 8x). Peeking round corners, crouching, going prone in grass. Healing with bandages, first aid kits, med kits; boosting with energy drinks and painkillers. Driving vehicles. Reviving a knocked-down teammate. Throwing frag grenades, smoke grenades, molotovs and stun grenades. Reloading. Emotes and spray paint. Firing a flare gun to call a special airdrop. |
| **Characters** | Player-created avatars, male or female, in customisable outfits. Cosmetic outfit sets range from plain military clothing to bright fantasy-style 'mythic' sets. Gear worn over the outfit: helmet level 1 to 3 (level 3 is a full-face military helmet), vest level 1 to 3, backpack level 1 to 3. A frying pan hangs on the back or hip and can block bullets. A ghillie suit (grass-covered camouflage) comes from airdrops. Knocked players slump to the ground and crawl. |
| **Environments and maps** | Erangel: a green grassland island with small towns of two-storey houses with red and grey roofs, a walled school compound, a coastal military base on a southern island reached by two long bridges, a power plant, a quarry, farms, hills and pine woods. Miramar: desert with canyons, sand, flat-roofed towns and an oil field. Sanhok: dense jungle, ruins and a boot camp. Vikendi: snow, a castle and a cosmodrome. Livik: a small map with waterfalls and a Nordic-style town. Karakin and Nusa: very small maps. |
| **Vehicles and items** | Vehicles: UAZ jeep (open-top or hard-top, olive green), Dacia sedan, buggy, motorcycle with or without sidecar, Mirado muscle car, scooter, boat, motor glider, and the BRDM amphibious armoured vehicle from a flare-gun airdrop. Vehicles have cosmetic skins. Weapons: M416, AKM, SCAR-L, M762, UMP45, Vector, Kar98k, M24, AWM (airdrop only), Groza (airdrop only), DBS, S686, crossbow, frying pan. Items: bandages, first aid kit, med kit, energy drink, painkillers, adrenaline syringe, smoke and frag grenades, molotov, flare gun. Airdrop crate: a wooden crate under a parachute, trailing red smoke. |
| **Signature moments** | The plane pass with dozens of players jumping at once. Chasing an airdrop by its red smoke. The final circle with players prone in tall grass. Reviving a knocked teammate inside a smoke cloud. A frying pan kill. A vehicle flipping on a hill. Camping the long bridge to the military base. A ghillie-suit sniper on a hilltop. The 'Winner Winner Chicken Dinner' result screen. |
| **Visual style** | Realistic. Natural, slightly muted colours. Real-world textures on buildings, grass, dirt and metal. Muted military look on gear. No gore: hit effects are green sparks by default in the India version. Soft daylight, dusk and night variants exist. No outlines, no cartoon shading. |
| **Camera and view** | Third person over the shoulder by default, zooming into a scope view when aiming. A first-person mode exists. Vehicles are seen from behind and above. Spectator and replay cameras exist. |
| **On-screen display** | Minimap top-right with the white safe-zone circle, the blue current-zone circle and the plane's path line. Health bar bottom-centre with a boost meter above it. Ammo count and fire mode bottom-right. Kill feed top-left. Compass strip top-centre. Alive-player counter top-right beside the minimap. Teammate health bars on the left. Touch controls (fire, jump, crouch, prone buttons) around the edges on mobile. |
| **Things this game does NOT have** | No building structures. No character special powers or magic. No cartoon or anime look. No double jump or wall running. No blood or gore. Real player names appear in the kill feed and above heads: they must be blurred or replaced in any picture we use. |

### Real Cricket 24

| Heading | Brief |
|---|---|
| **How a match works** | The player picks a format: T20, one-day (50 overs), Test, or a tournament such as a world cup or a franchise T20 league. A coin toss decides who bats first. The batting side scores runs; the bowling side takes wickets. An over is six balls. In limited-overs formats each side bats once; in Tests each side bats twice. The match ends with a result screen and, for tournaments, a trophy presentation with confetti. The player controls batting shots, bowling deliveries and some fielding; the rest is automatic. |
| **Core actions** | Batting: choose a shot and direction and time it: straight drive, cover drive, pull, hook, sweep, reverse sweep, late cut, lofted drive, helicopter shot, uppercut, scoop, switch hit, defensive block, leave. Running between wickets and sliding the bat over the crease. Bowling: pick a length and type: yorker, bouncer, slower ball, inswinger, outswinger, off-spin, leg-spin, googly, doosra. Fielding: diving stop, boundary catch, relay throw, direct-hit run-out, wicketkeeper stumping. Appealing to the umpire. Asking for a third-umpire review with ball tracking and an edge-detection soundwave graphic. |
| **Characters** | Realistic-looking cricketers in team kit: coloured kits with numbers and names on the back in limited-overs cricket, whites in Tests. Helmet with a face grille, pads, gloves, thigh guard. Bowlers in caps or bare-headed. Two on-field umpires in dark trousers, white shirt and a wide-brim hat, plus a third umpire off-field. Some real players and teams are licensed; treat every face and name as off-limits in our prompts and use fictional team names. |
| **Environments and maps** | Modern cricket stadiums: an oval grass outfield, a central pitch with painted creases, three stumps at each end, sightscreens behind each end, a boundary rope lined with advertising boards, a big screen, floodlight towers, a dressing-room balcony, and stands full of spectators with flags. Conditions: day, day-night (sunset turning into floodlights), night; clear, overcast, or rain with covers pulled over the pitch. |
| **Vehicles and items** | Bat (wooden, with a sponsor-free blade in our prompts), red ball (Tests), white ball (limited overs), pink ball (day-night Tests), stumps with LED bails and stumps that light up red when hit, sightscreen, drinks trolley, coin for the toss, trophy, fireworks and confetti at presentations. |
| **Signature moments** | A helicopter shot for six. The stumps lighting up when a batter is bowled. A diving one-handed boundary catch. The third-umpire replay: slow motion of the ball passing the bat, the soundwave spike, then the ball-tracking line and pitch map. A direct-hit run-out with the batter diving full length. The bowler's wicket celebration and team huddle. A century celebration: helmet off, bat raised to the crowd. A super over or last-ball finish. The trophy lift. |
| **Visual style** | Realistic television broadcast look. Bright, even stadium lighting; deep green outfield; crisp white creases; saturated kit colours. Broadcast graphics: score bar along the bottom, bowling speed readout, wagon wheel, pitch map, ball-tracking line. Slow-motion replays with a replay banner. |
| **Camera and view** | Broadcast camera behind and above the bowler's arm for each delivery. Behind-the-batter view for shots. Side-on camera for run-outs at the crease. Low stump camera. Sweeping crowd and stadium shots between overs. Slow-motion replay cameras. A top-down field-setting screen shows all eleven fielders as dots with position names. |
| **On-screen display** | Score bar bottom-centre: team score and wickets, overs, run rate, target or required rate. Bowling speed readout after release. Batting shot indicator and timing meter. Fielding prompt during a catch or dive. Batter and bowler name plates (fictional names only in our prompts). Between-over summary graphics and the wagon wheel. |
| **Things this game does NOT have** | No fantasy powers or arcade effects. No cartoon look. No first-person view. No crowd running onto the pitch. Real player faces and names are licensed and must not appear in our prompts or outputs; use fictional team names and generic players. |
